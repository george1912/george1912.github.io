Why use this

Study mode, not screen-watching

ATI pages can time out if you step away or take notes. Fox Timer quietly keeps the session alive.

Use that time to focus: read, take notes, review, and actually learn — instead of clicking around just to keep the page from logging you out.

How to use

After it’s installed

  1. 1 Open your ATI lesson, video, assessment, or Real Life popup in Chrome.
  2. 2 Click the fox icon (puzzle piece → pin Fox Timer if you don’t see it).
  3. 3 Pick what you’re doing, enter minutes, press Play.
  4. 4 Leave that window open and study. Pause or End anytime.
Tip: start with a short timer (5–10 minutes) the first time, so you can see how it feels before a longer study block. Not sure which dropdown to pick? See use cases →
