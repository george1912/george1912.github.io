Brightspace Sync

Pin your classes. Update once. Files on your Mac.

Syncs whatever you have pinned in Brightspace into a folder you choose — and can turn Word and PowerPoint into PDFs when you want them.

Download for Mac How it works

Apple Silicon · macOS · Pin-first sync · v1.0.2

What it can do

  1. 1

    Syncs your pins

    Whatever is pinned in Brightspace gets updated. Unpin last term; pin this term.

  2. 2

    Archive old folders

    When Summer classes are no longer pinned, one tap moves their folders to Archive — nothing deleted.

  3. 3

    Optional PDFs

    Converts new or missing Office files with LibreOffice — originals stay put.

Install in a minute

  1. Open the DMG and drag Brightspace Sync to Applications.
  2. Open the app. If macOS blocks it, right-click → Open once.
  3. In Brightspace, pin this term’s classes (unpin last term).
  4. In the app: Update → sign in → Archive old folders if offered.

Good to know

  • Built for nursing school workflows — pins, quiet updates, optional PDF catch-up.
  • PDF conversion needs LibreOffice installed.
  • Unsigned personal build for now: Gatekeeper may ask you to confirm Open the first time.
